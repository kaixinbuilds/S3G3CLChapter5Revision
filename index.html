一款教「对比论证法」的拖曳游戏，三关共 36 分。由一位华文老师与 AI 对话做成，不需要任何编程基础。

A drag-and-drop game that teaches 对比论证法 across three levels and 36 marks — built by a Chinese Language teacher in conversation with AI, with no coding background.

🎮 玩游戏 Play the game 三关 10 个版面，36 分。拖曳作答，手机、iPad、电脑都能玩。 Three levels, 10 panels, 36 marks. Drag to answer; works on phone, iPad and computer. 进入游戏 → Start playing →

给学生的两句话：请在 SLS 里作答，不要另开分页，否则分数记录不到；三关做完会出现登顶画面，请自行截图交到 SLS。

Two things students need to know: answer inside SLS rather than in a new tab, or the score cannot be recorded; and screenshot the summit screen at the end to submit as proof.
